Plain-language policy

Privacy

Your task notes stay in this browser unless you export them.

What this app stores

Your current card, history, reminder settings, and chosen print style are saved in this browser. The demo flow makes only same-origin requests. Exported files go only where you choose to save them.

Demo data

The demo uses a separate browser store named demo:next-step-cards. It does not read or change your cards. Reset demo replaces only that sample store. Start for real clears the sample store.

Reminders

Choose a reminder time and quiet hours in Manage data and settings. You can change notification permission in browser or device settings.

Network and analytics

The demo flow makes only same-origin requests. The hosting provider may keep ordinary security and access logs.

Delete or move your data

Use Manage data and settings to export JSON or CSV and clear history. To remove all cards, clear this site’s browser storage. Uninstalling alone may not clear browser storage.

Changes and contact

This policy is effective 28 August 2026. Material changes appear on this page. Questions can be sent through sociobot.in.
